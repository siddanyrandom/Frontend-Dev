Rich History

Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is known for its timeless temples, shrines, and gardens that date back over a thousand years.

Traditional Culture

From tea ceremonies to kimono art, Kyoto preserves Japanese culture with grace and authenticity.

Famous Attractions

Don’t miss the Golden Pavilion, Fushimi Inari Shrine, and the peaceful Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.

Cuisine & Flavors

Kyoto’s cuisine emphasizes harmony, balance, and seasonal ingredients, from matcha sweets to kaiseki meals.
